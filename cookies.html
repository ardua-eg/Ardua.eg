Cookie policy

This website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not load anything from other companies while you browse. Because of that, there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

What is stored on your device

The website remembers what is in your cart, so it is still there if you refresh or come back later. It does this with one small item in your browser's local storage:

  • Name: ardua.cart.v1
  • What it holds: the caps in your cart and the quantity of each
  • Why: the cart cannot work without it, so it is strictly necessary and needs no consent
  • How long: until you check out, remove the items, or clear your browser data

It never contains your name, phone number or address, and it is never sent to us until you place an order.

What we do not use

  • no analytics
  • no advertising or social media pixels
  • no embedded videos, maps or social feeds
  • no fonts or scripts loaded from other websites; everything comes from our own site

When data leaves this website

The only time your browser contacts another company is when you press "Place order". It then sends your order to our form service, Formspree. See the privacy policy.

Your website host

Like every website, our host receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, and keeps it in server logs for security and operation.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising or other optional tools, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
