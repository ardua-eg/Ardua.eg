Cookie Policy

A plain-language explanation of browser storage and tracking on this store.

Essential browser storage

ARDUA uses localStorage (a browser storage feature, not a traditional HTTP cookie) to remember your cart and your cookie preference. These features are needed for the shopping experience to work.

Optional analytics

No analytics or advertising trackers are currently enabled. The consent panel includes an optional analytics preference for future use, but choosing it does not activate any analytics on the current site.

Your choice

You can choose “Essential only” in the consent panel, or reopen Cookie settings from the footer at any time. Clearing site data in your browser will also clear the saved cart and consent preference.

Third-party requests

The store does not load third-party analytics scripts. Product photos and site assets are served from this website. If third-party services are added later, this policy and the consent behavior should be updated before they are enabled.
