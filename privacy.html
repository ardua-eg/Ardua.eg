Privacy Policy

How ARDUA handles information you provide when placing an order.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the contact and delivery details you submit (name, email, phone number, address and city), plus the items ordered, quantities, total and order reference. We ask only for information needed to handle the order and delivery.

How we use it

We use order details to receive and manage your order, contact you about delivery fees and timing, respond to questions, prevent misuse, and keep appropriate business records.

Order processing provider

Order submissions are sent to our configured order-form service (Formspree) so the store operator can receive order details. That provider processes submitted information under its own terms and privacy practices. Do not submit payment-card details through this form.

Storage and security

Your cart is stored in your browser using local storage so it can persist between visits. Order details are sent to the order-processing provider. We take reasonable steps to limit data collection, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Cookies and analytics

This page does not load advertising pixels or analytics tools. Essential browser storage is used for cart functionality and remembering your cookie choice. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Retention and your choices

Order information may be kept for as long as reasonably needed for order support, recordkeeping and legal obligations. To ask about your information or request correction/deletion where applicable, contact ARDUA using the contact details in your order confirmation or store communications.

Children and updates

The store is not designed to knowingly collect information from children without appropriate permission. We may update this policy as the store changes; the latest version will be posted on this page.

This page is general store information, not legal advice. Please review it against your actual business practices and applicable law before launch.
