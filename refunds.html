Refund & Returns Policy

What to do if an order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect.

If there is a problem

Please contact ARDUA promptly using the contact details in your order confirmation or store communications. Include your order number and a description of the issue; photos may help us assess damage or an incorrect item.

Damaged, defective or incorrect orders

We will review the issue and discuss an appropriate remedy in line with applicable consumer protection law. Depending on the circumstances and applicable rules, this may include replacement, repair, refund or another remedy.

Change-of-mind requests

Please contact us before returning any item. Return eligibility may depend on the product condition, timing and applicable law. Do not send an item back before receiving return instructions.

Refund timing

If a refund is agreed, we will confirm the method and expected timing with you. For cash-on-delivery orders, the refund arrangement will be agreed directly.

Legal rights preserved

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or limit any non-waivable consumer rights under applicable Egyptian law or other applicable law.
